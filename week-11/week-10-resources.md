## ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Standard

| Resource | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 (official)** | Third edition — purchase from iso.org or national standards body |
| **ISO/IEC 27000:2018** | ISMS vocabulary and definitions — freely available from ISO |
| **Transition deadline** | ISO 27001:2013 certifications expired October 31, 2025 (IAF resolution) |
| **What changed summary** | 93 controls (vs 114 in 2013); 4 themes (A.5–A.8); Clause 6.3 added; 11 new controls |

**New controls in 2022 (not in 2013) — relevant to GRC:**
